# Job Copilot MVP Spec

## Goal
Build a lightweight single-user job search copilot for Mayank Gulati.

## Core functions
1. Score jobs for fit
2. Recommend best resume variant
3. Flag open-ended application questions
4. Draft answers for review
5. Recommend outreach targets
6. Draft outreach messages for review
7. Track applications and update status from Gmail confirmations
8. Sync to Notion

## Role families
- Strategic Operations
- Business Operations
- Revenue Operations
- Sales Strategy & Operations
- Chief of Staff / Office of COO
- CX / Enablement / Transformation

## Resume variants
- master_2026
- revops_q
- strategic_ops_a
- cx_c
- cx_ops_l
- startup_builder_sb

## Rules
- Never auto-submit open-ended answers
- Never auto-send outreach
- Gmail confirmation = successful application
